# <img src="./frontend/assets/saeif-logo.png" alt="SAEIF logo" height="35"> SAEIF

Welcome to **SAEIF (Smart Emergency and Triage System)**, an intelligent emergency department management system designed to improve triage accuracy, patient safety, and hospital operational efficiency. SAEIF combines **Artificial Intelligence (AI), Internet of Things (IoT), predictive analytics, and automated SMS communication** into one unified platform to support healthcare staff and improve the patient experience.

## 🏥 System Components
**AI-Powered Triage:** 
Machine learning models analyze patient symptoms and vital signs to classify patients according to CTAS levels and support healthcare professionals in making faster, data-driven decisions.

**IoT-Based Monitoring:**
Wearable devices continuously collect patient vital signs. The system analyzes the incoming data and generates alerts when signs of deterioration are detected.

**Crowding Prediction:**
AI models analyze historical emergency department data together with factors such as seasonal trends, weather, and local events to forecast periods of high demand.

**Patient Communication:**
An automated SMS system provides patients with personalized updates about their estimated waiting times and queue status.

## 💡 Features

- **AI-Powered Triage:** Classifies patients according to the Canadian Triage and Acuity Scale (CTAS) using machine learning models to support faster and more consistent triage decisions.
- **Real-Time Patient Monitoring:** Uses IoT wearable devices to continuously monitor vital signs such as heart rate, body temperature, and oxygen saturation for CTAS level 2–3 patients.
- **Deterioration Alerts:** Automatically detects abnormal changes in monitored patients and alerts medical staff for timely intervention.
- **ED Crowding Prediction:** Predicts emergency department demand using historical patient data, seasonal trends, weather conditions, and local events.
- **Operational Decision Support:** Provides insights that help hospitals with proactive staff planning, resource allocation, and patient flow management.
- **SMS Notifications:** Sends patients real-time updates about estimated waiting times and queue status.
- **Unified Healthcare Dashboard:** Provides healthcare staff with access to triage classifications, patient vital signs, alerts, and crowding predictions in one platform.

- **Database:** Firebase
- **IoT:** ESP32 microcontroller and MAX30102 Sensor
